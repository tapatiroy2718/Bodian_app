Bodiana ("we", "us") runs the Bodiana mobile app, a wedding planning and vendor marketplace. This policy explains what we collect and why.

## Information we collect

- **Account information:** name, email address, optional phone number and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and profile picture from Google.
- **Wedding planning information (customers):** wedding date, location, budget, guest count, events, checklist items, and provider details you enter.
- **Business information (vendors):** business name, category, address, city, description, contact numbers, photos, services, packages, prices and availability.
- **Verification documents (vendors):** trade licence, registration or other documents you upload. Only you and our verification team can see these. They are never shown to customers.
- **Bookings and reviews:** booking requests, the contact number you share with a vendor, status changes, and reviews you write.
- **Technical data:** basic logs our hosting provider needs to run the service securely.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts or payment card details.

## How we use it

To create your account, show the marketplace, send booking requests between customers and vendors, verify vendor businesses, send in-app notifications, prevent fraud and keep the service secure.

## Who we share it with

- **Between users:** when a customer sends a booking request, the vendor sees the customer's name, phone number and request details. Vendor business details are public in the marketplace once the vendor is verified. Reviews show the reviewer's name.
- **Service providers:** Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) and Google (if you use Google sign-in), which process data on our behalf.
- We do not sell personal data.

## Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account). This permanently deletes your profile, plans, bookings, services and uploads. You can also request deletion by emailing [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access rules ensure each user can only reach their own private data.

## Children

Bodiana is intended for users aged 18 and over.
